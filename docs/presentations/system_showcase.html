10Trust by design

Built for decisions people can defend in an audit.

01

No LLM arithmetic

Every figure comes from a tool: pandas, the exact score formula or rule-based what-if. The model selects and explains.

02

Cited evidence

Inline sources on every claim: [data], [doc: file], [email: subject], [web: url].

03

Honest labels

Country, category, fleets, history and orders are enrichment, labelled SIMULATED wherever they appear.

04

Human in the loop

Recommended actions are marked "need approval". Recommendations require risk < 50; the buyer's priority adjusts the ranking but never overrides risk.

05

Graceful fallbacks

Regex for emails, keyword matching for requests, a persona template for the UI. The app still works when a model call fails.

06

Bounded agents

Step caps (6 / 4 / 3 / 2 per agent), tool output capped at 12k chars, caches for OCR and Tavily, thinking off for latency.
